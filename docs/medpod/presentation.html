02 · The gap

Hold sits inside the ~72-hour PCC window. The gap is solo, not duration.

Nearly every intervention that improves survival in prolonged care either requires a second person — or becomes actively dangerous performed alone.

Doctrinal fit

72 hours

  • Matches the JTS PCC working ceiling
  • Antibiotic prophylaxis ≤72 h ends with the hold
  • Prior art: Role 2 ~48 h — stretch is ~1.5×, not weeks
Still uncovered

Solo care

  • Doctrine assumes a caregiver for critical acts
  • Self-applied tourniquet failure without outcome checks
  • Only 14% arrive Role II/III with prehospital care documented
Why it matters: Stretch vs Role 2 (~1.5×) is modest; the uncovered gap is solo care — second-person acts and undocumented prehospital care (D1).

04 · MedPod · Three inversions

The intuitive capsule is wrong in three quantified ways.

Inversion 1 · Sealing
~34 min

to 5% CO₂ in a sealed pod

About an hour to unconsciousness. Ventilation is the primary life-safety system — fail-open, zero power. Nothing else kills this fast.

Inversion 2 · Heat
0 W

active heating to ≈ −10 °C

Metabolic heat + 53 mm foam over the body and 85 mm beneath closes the balance. Chemical heat was always a 10-hour answer to a 72-hour problem (~7× short).

Inversion 3 · Real threat
42–48 mmHg

pelvis / heels on bare litter

Pressure injury, not cold, dominates. Stage 3–4 forms well inside a week. The best insulator — a vacuum mattress — is among the worst support surfaces.

Why it matters: Product order flips: ventilation integrity and pressure management first; warming almost free once insulation is right (D2 three inversions).

05 · MedPod · Life support

A sealed pod is a coffin on a one-hour timer.

  • 0.22 L/min CO₂ from a resting 80 kg adult into ~150–400 L free air — occupational 0.5% in ~7 min; 5% intoxication in ~34 min; LOC at ~10% / ~68 min
  • Required ventilation: 0.5–2 L/s per casualty, fail-open, no closeable valve — structurally impossible to seal, not instrumented
  • Face at hooded aperture so exhaled CO₂ leaves without mixing; pod then needs only trickle ventilation
  • Counterflow recuperator (~70% effective) drops the 12.6 W trickle penalty to ~3.8 W; HME recovers ~11.5 W and 287 g water/day
  • Excluded: combustion / catalytic heaters inside the volume; air-activated chemical warmers add ~36% to O₂ demand

06 · MedPod · Coupled constraints

Three coupled constraints — how the design answers.

1 · CO₂ / ventilation

Seal vs breathe

Problem: sealed ~150 L → ~34 min to 5% CO₂, ~1 h LOC; fastest killer.

Design: fail-open vents (no closeable valve); face at hooded aperture; trickle 0.5–2 L/s; counterflow recuperator (~70%) so vent doesn’t steal heat; HME recovers respiratory heat/water.

Hard rule: power must never be required to breathe; powered tier still fail-open.

2 · Heating need

72 h vs chemical heat

Problem: HPMK chemical heat ~10 h vs 72 h (~7× short); air-activated warmers raise O₂ demand; combustion/catalytic inside = CO (no exit).

Design: insulation first — ~53 mm foam over / ~85 mm under closes balance on ~70 W metabolism to ≈ −10 °C with 0 W active heat; electrical heat only supplemental (powered tier) below ≈ −10 °C or collapsed metabolism, energy preferably outside occupied volume.

Thesis: active heat is last resort — never the product.

3 · Thermal signature

Warmth vs concealment

Problem: hypothermia doctrine wants warmth; concealment hates a hot blob; Mylar-outward / aluminium-out creates a cold rectangle on warm ground — often more conspicuous.

Design: same insulation grams buy survival and smaller ΔT to ambient; radiant barrier metal face inward across air gap (never outward as camouflage); matte outer shell; no reflective sunshade as primary cold-signature strategy; screen-off / deep-red UI for phone (KR-04).

Buy: survival + smaller ΔT with the same grams.

Bottom line: Insulation settles the heat–signature conflict; fail-open vent settles the seal–life conflict; active heat is last resort, never the product thesis.

07 · MedPod · Thermal & pressure

Insulation, not chemical heat. Pressure injury dominates.

Thermal

~70 W metabolic · 24 °C interior

  • −10 °C: 53 mm over body / 85 mm beneath (XLPE-eq.)
  • −25 °C: ~80 mm / 128 mm — Arctic is a thickness decision
  • Base needs ~1.6× canopy R-value; ground is the sink
  • Blizzard bag 7.5 TOG ≈ 26 mm — ~⅓ of −10 °C need
  • Signature free: outer ΔT only 1.3–3.2 K; never metallise outward
Pressure

Vacuum mattress tension

  • Bare litter: pelvis ~42 mmHg, heels ~48 mmHg
  • “32 mmHg is safe” is a documented myth
  • No published unattended multi-day pressure solution (still critical inside 72 h)
  • Baseline: zoned foam + sacral/heel relief + mouth-inflatable tilt bladders (~USD 16, 300 g)
  • Largest technical risk in the concept remains
Why it matters: Insulation closes the heat balance on ~70 W metabolism to ≈ −10 °C; chemical heat was a 10 h answer. Pressure injury (not cold) dominates inside 72 h — tilt bladders make nursing possible alone (D1 KR-08 / D2).

10 · MedPod · Kit

Kit — medicines and instruments (72 h hold).

Separate from structural BOM · Fixed-dose cards only · Cite TCCC / JTS · Surface conflicts; do not resolve

Medicines · 72 h / 3 days

Fixed-dose only

  • Cefadroxil 1 g PO once daily × 3 — TCCC 01 May 2026. Conflict: JTS ID 24 still names moxifloxacin — surface both; do not silently pick.
  • Analgesia: pre-portioned daily pouches × 3 (physical constraint, not a software ceiling). TCCC CWMP path; no opioids invented here.
  • Enoxaparin 30 mg BD once haemostasis achieved and >48 h expected — JTS CPG ID 36; only if criteria met.
  • TXA / other controlled acute resus drugs omitted from this kit page — not listed as MedPod hold field-kit line items in D1.
Instruments / consumables

Self-care + handover

  • Combat-application tourniquet × ≥1 (limb)
  • Haemostatic / packing gauze
  • Splint (distal pulse checks)
  • External thermometer (iPhone has none — kit line item)
  • Graduated urine measure (also structural BOM interface)
  • Fitbit or equiv BLE HR — MedPod default monitoring; trend only; SpO₂ not clinical
  • Spare HME cartridges
  • Oral care kit (KR-08 nursing/morale)
  • Buddy-contact / handover print or QR-capable display path
Not a Role 2 pharmacy. PoC ships fixed-dose protocol cards only — no dose calculation in the app. Fixed doses + calculation firewall are safety architecture for PoC, not store-compliance theatre. Content is versioned and attributed; conflicts stay visible.

12 · App MVP · Six capabilities

A clock and a ledger before it is a teacher.

C1

The Clock

Tourniquet 2 h / 6 h gates, drug intervals, q1–2 h repositioning, q6 h splint checks, dressing age. The irreplaceable function.

C2

The Ledger

Append-only clinical record — interventions, doses, measurements — timestamps from the device, not from failing memory.

C3

Outcome-gated guidance

Closed-set procedural cards gated on observable outcomes (e.g. distal pulse), not step confirmation.

C4

Self-measurement

Pulse count, urine volume, radial pulse Y/N, timed cognitive task vs personal baseline — four that survive self-admin.

C6

Psychological continuity

Nursing-as-morale, extraction log without shame (KR-07/08), timed cognitive vs baseline, procedural presence checks — continuity, not therapy.

C5

The Handover — DD Form 1380 / MIST

Packet on demand and on dead-man trigger. Readable by a medic with no app and no network — screen and QR. Strongest product argument; cheap to build.

Why it matters: Trustable clock/ledger before “smart” AI — evidence and trust gaps argue measure-first PoC; agents that follow up when no medic is present map to prompts, not replacement of care (KB / D3 KR-01).

17 · Survivability claims

Survivability — what we claim and what we refuse.

No validated “X% more survive with MedPod + app.” Claiming one would be marketing. Gain = avoided specific failure modes.

WILL say (PoC)

Scoped mechanisms only

  • Applies to casualties who already cleared the acute filter (conscious, haemorrhage controlled enough to self-treat)
  • Targets post-acute killers doctrine ignores for solo care: asphyxia-from-sealing, hypothermia from thin bags / 10 h chemical heat, missed nursing clocks, self-aid TQ errors, missing handover (only 14% documented)
  • Pod+app can improve odds on those mechanisms; measure proxies in PoC
WILL NOT say

No blanket % · no Role 2 replace

  • A single survivability % for all battlefield wounded
  • Fixes truncal/junctional haemorrhage (~majority of potentially survivable bleeds)
  • Eliminates pressure injury (largest residual risk)
  • Replaces Role 2 / surgery; or treats psych/suicide (no CPG)
~2.6% · extremity HE Of all battlefield deaths ≈ extremity haemorrhage a lone casualty might self-treat (Eastridge stack · D1)
~63–65% · self-TQ Lower-limb self-TQ success; common error = skip distal pulse (D1)
~1 in 5 · UGV UGV retrieval success; extraction recurring (KR-07)
~10%→~30% Evac stretch mortality is about time to surgery — not this product
PoC measure: hypothermia on recovery · Stage 2+ sores by 72 h · TQ verified occlusion · prophylaxis completed · handover completeness — then model a delta, don’t invent one.
Why it matters: Evidence gap (KB: AI health tools under-evaluated) — PoC measures, does not claim clinical efficacy.

18 · Gates & open questions

PoC learning goals — measure before claiming.

SAFE

Model must not unsupervised-decide

Safety architecture, not store policy: human confirmation on every clinically consequential action; language proposes, Swift commits. Unsupervised clinical AI is refused in PoC.

BENCH

Ventilation & thermal bench

Prove fail-open CO₂ path and insulation balance with measurements — every MedPod number is still a calculation until bench. Prior art max ~48 h Role 2; HPMK heat 10 h; 72 h is stretch, not fantasy.

UX

Clock + ledger + Fitbit session + psych continuity

Prove trustable timers/ledger before “smart” features; Fitbit ≤60 s trend sessions; six psych artefacts without therapy claims. Evidence gap: PoC must measure, not claim efficacy.

DEFER

Post-PoC only

App Store / publisher / EU MDR class · powered-tier Wh sizing · AutoLoader geometry · distribution. Fixed-dose firewall stays; store gate is not the PoC hardest problem.
